## Quick Guide on 8BitDo SN30 Pro Joystick Replacement

To keep it quick and simple, this guide will go through a step-by-step process on how to replace the old and crusty joystick with a new and silky smooth one (do note that I'm not replacing the switches, but only the thumbgrip/joystick cap thingy)

### Why This Compatibility is Important?

Compatibility is very important because if the joysticks are slightly bigger than the original, they might touch the actual case around the joystick hole. This creates friction that makes the joystick stick to the case, making it harder to actually use the joystick. *(Check the explanation in the video)*

## Replacement Steps

### 1. Unscrew the back cover

![Unscrew Back Cover](/assets/img/posts/sn30pro-joystick-replace/capture4.jpeg)
_Unscrew Back Cover_

Unscrew the back cover. There are 5 total screws for the back cover. The screws are called **Torx T6 Screws**

### 2. Unscrew the breakout board of the joystick switch

![Unscrew Breakout Board](/assets/img/posts/sn30pro-joystick-replace/capture5.jpeg)
_Unscrew Breakout Board_

Unscrew the breakout board, it is held down by 4 screws shown in the picture.

### 3. Pull the battery from the tape

![Remove Battery From Tape](/assets/img/posts/sn30pro-joystick-replace/capture6.jpeg)
_Remove Battery From Tape_

> **Be very careful** when trying to pull out the battery to not get it punctured
{: .prompt-warning}

Pull out the battery from the tape.

### 4. Pull out the breakout board and the joystick (be careful)

![Pull Out The Breakout Board](/assets/img/posts/sn30pro-joystick-replace/capture7.jpeg)
_Pull Out The Breakout Board_

> **Be very careful** with the breakout board ribbon cable
{: .prompt-warning}

Pull out the breakout board to reveal the joystick.

### 5. Replace the joystick

![Replace The Joystick](/assets/img/posts/sn30pro-joystick-replace/capture8.jpeg)
_Replace The Joystick_

Replace the original joystick with the compatible one, then screw the breakout board and back cover back on (basically repeating steps 1 & 2)

### 6. Finish

![Replaced Joystick](/assets/img/posts/sn30pro-joystick-replace/capture9.jpeg)
_Replaced Joystick_

**AND IT'S DONE!**
